# Scope & Pay

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent developers, designers and small studios serving clients directly.
Their clients review and explicitly accept changes to agreed work and prices.
The user selected this audience and workflow as idea 1 on 2026-10-15.

## Product Purpose

Connect a versioned project brief and conversation evidence to a reviewed change
proposal, explicit client acceptance and the corresponding PayPal invoice/payment.
Help distinguish included revisions, requested additions and unresolved questions.

## Operating Context

Existing FastAPI, PostgreSQL and React application. PayPal account access and
OpenAI credentials are not configured. Local sample review and invoice simulation
must be labelled; external providers must report their actual state.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing session security, tenant isolation, payment evidence and local
operations history. New scope/invoice models are separate from access repairs.
AI proposes interpretations with evidence references; people choose prices and
clients accept exact proposal versions. Silence or AI output cannot be consent.
One currency (USD) in the first complete workflow. No tax advice, escrow, payroll
or automatic collections. Real provider validation remains a release gate.

## Brand Commitments

User asks for a basic, classic, organized interface with readable type, familiar
labels and no flashy effects. Scope & Pay is a descriptive working name chosen
for this build, not a user-approved final trademark.
The approved visual reference is PayPal's sandbox dashboard: a pale sidebar,
white content, strong black typography, pill buttons and blue navigation states.
Apply this consistently to the workspace and client review while preserving
Scope & Pay branding and showing only real project totals.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing durable-job, approval, recovery and PayPal read contracts. Synthetic
project examples only; no merchant demand, testimonials or saved-revenue claims.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the source behind each proposed change.
- Make the next meaningful decision clear.
- Bind acceptance and billing to exact versions and amounts.
- Distinguish local demonstration from genuine provider results.
